## SUPERVISOR AUTHORIZATION — P05 READINESS AUDIT

The Product Owner does **not** need to fill the form again. The conversation has already supplied enough direction to begin the definition/readiness work.

Treat the following as captured Product Owner input:

### Captured product direction

**Bible selection / first-run**
- On first use, the app should ask the user to choose between **Protestant** and **Catholic**.
- Protestant target translation: **Reina-Valera 1960**.
- Catholic target: a **recognized Catholic Bible with the Catholic canon**.
- The exact Catholic edition is not yet selected. Implementer must research/recommend a suitable candidate and verify exact edition/canon/versification/source/redistribution rights before any corpus is integrated.
- RVR1960 must also pass a real redistribution/license gate before full text is bundled. Do not assume personal availability equals redistribution permission.
- If rights are not available, prepare the architecture and report the content/license blocker rather than using an unauthorized corpus.

**Notes / reflections**
- While reading Scripture, the user must be able to tap/select a verse and open a small note/reflection UI.
- The user writes a reflection and saves it locally.
- Implementer should recommend the smallest coherent edit/delete/reopen behavior and where notes are surfaced later (verse + Library) without asking the Product Owner to redesign the feature.

**Spotify / Christian music**
- RC02 should include **Christian Spotify playlists**.
- Exact UX placement is intentionally delegated: compare a dedicated section/tab, card in Hoy, Library placement, button/widget, or another simple pattern.
- Recommend the placement that best preserves Bible-first navigation and does not make Spotify a dependency of the core app.
- Evaluate first version as playlist discovery/open-in-Spotify versus deeper SDK playback. Prefer the simplest reliable option unless evidence shows deeper integration is materially better.
- Research/propose concrete playlist categories and candidate playlists for later approval; do not invent availability or policy support.

**Reminder**
- A reminder feature is desired, but its exact purpose/copy/deep-link target has not been frozen.
- Implementer must propose a coherent reminder behavior tied to the app's core job (for example daily Scripture / continue reading) and explain why.
- It must remain optional, user-controllable and local/best-effort; do not promise exact alarm semantics.

**Calma viva**
- Preserve the already stated direction: calm, serenity, encouragement, purity; less green dominance; warmer rose/mauve/peach/lavender family; light + dark; accessible and readable.

**APK policy**
- Do **not** generate APKs for routine microchanges.
- Use source-level tests, typecheck, deterministic checks and CI first.
- Generate an APK only when a candidate has passed prior gates, or when a concrete Android runtime question cannot be resolved credibly without an APK.
- Final physical validation remains on the Human's phone; local emulator/ADB is optional acceleration, not canonical development authority.

### Delegated authority for this activity

The Implementer is expected to resolve **subordinate product/UX/technical choices** through research and reasoning instead of returning every detail to the Product Owner.

Only escalate if a remaining question materially changes:
- religious/canon direction;
- licensing/redistribution posture;
- privacy/account/cloud model;
- major navigation/product identity;
- monetization/business model;
- or another comparable product-level decision.
